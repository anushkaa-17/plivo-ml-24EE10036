ML Speedrun Submission Summary

1. Architecture & Parameters

The final model is a custom GPT architecture carefully scaled to roughly 1.64 million parameters (safely under the 2M cap). Key modifications include:

  • SwiGLU Activation: Replaced the standard MLP with a SwiGLU block for richer data representation per parameter.
  • Weight Tying: Shared weights between the input tok_emb and output lm_head to reclaim ~100k parameters, allowing for a wider embedding dimension.
  • BPE Tokenization: Upgraded from a byte-level tokenizer to Byte-Pair Encoding (vocab 512) to efficiently compress multi-byte Hindi characters.
  • Optimizer: AdamW (lr=6e-4) with a Cosine Decay schedule and Linear Warmup.
  • Zero Dropout: Removed dropout entirely, as regularization is unnecessary and counterproductive given the strict 2,000-step training limit.

2. Why This is the Best Approach

This configuration maximizes learning efficiency within the hard constraints of the speedrun. Standard models waste parameters on redundant embeddings and waste steps mitigating overfitting (dropout). By removing dropout, employing Weight Tying, and using SwiGLU, every single parameter and training step is strictly dedicated to decreasing the Bits-Per-Byte (BPB) loss on the specific mixed-language text distribution.

3. Human vs. Machine Collaboration

This submission was a collaborative effort between human intuition and AI coding capabilities. The Human acted as the Principal Investigator: defining hypotheses, managing the hard constraints (monitoring the 2M parameter cap), identifying optimization bottlenecks (like the 2,000-step dropout issue), and executing the iterative scientific method in RUNLOG.md. The AI Agent acted as the lead engineer: rapidly generating the PyTorch boilerplate for complex algorithms like the BPE tokenizer logic, the SwiGLU implementation, and the AdamW training loop.
